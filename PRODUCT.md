## Users

International B2B buyers of clove: importers, distributors, and industrial manufacturers (food, pharma, cosmetics) evaluating a new Indonesian supplier and deciding whether to request a price offer.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Nusajawa Clove, an exporter that grows, processes, and exports clove from its own plantations in Java. Success = a qualified buyer submits an enquiry (product, volume, destination country).

## Positioning

Grower-exporter, not a broker: owns and manages its plantations in East Java (centre: Nganjuk), so it has full traceability from tree to shipment and is accountable for every lot. Every lot is tested before shipment; no shipment without complete documentation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Products: exactly two, Whole Clove Select (Super Grade A) and Whole Clove Standard (Grade A). No clove oil (user confirmed). Specs and MOQs as given in the brief.
- Annual capacity 20 T, eugenol ≥85% (Select), 15+ export markets.
- Documents: Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Analysis (per batch), Halal (MUI), Certificate of Origin, Health Certificate (on request).
- Packaging: 50 kg jute sack, 500 kg jumbo bag, custom/OEM. Loading port: Surabaya only.
- Own plantations in Nganjuk (owned and managed by the company, user confirmed); all supply comes from East Java.
- Enquiry form has no backend yet; submission target is undecided.
- Language: English only.
