Questions buyers ask

Short answers about our Indonesian clove: origin, grades, orders, shipping and documents.

Where do your cloves come from?

Our cloves come from East Java, Indonesia. We grow them on plantations we own and manage in Nganjuk, and our office is in Kediri. Every lot is traceable from tree to shipment.

Which clove grades do you export?

Two grades of whole clove. Select (Super Grade A): moisture 10% or less, eugenol 85% or more, essential oil 18% or more, foreign matter 1% or less. Standard (Grade A): moisture 12% or less, eugenol 72% or more, essential oil 15% or more.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The minimum order is 1 MT per grade. Annual capacity is 20 T.

How are the cloves packed and shipped?

Standard packing is a 50 kg jute sack; bulk orders ship in 500 kg jumbo bags, and custom or OEM packing is available on request. All cargo is loaded at the port of Surabaya.

Which documents come with each shipment?

Phytosanitary Certificate (Ministry of Agriculture), Certificate of Analysis per batch, Halal Certificate (MUI), Certificate of Origin (Chamber of Commerce), and a Health Certificate on request. Every lot is tested by our in-house team and an accredited third-party laboratory before it ships.

How do I get a price for Indonesian cloves?

Send the grade, monthly volume and destination country through the enquiry form, by email to export@nusajawa.id, or on WhatsApp. Our export team replies within one business day with pricing, specifications and current stock.

Which countries do you export to?

We serve importers, distributors and manufacturers in 15+ countries, including Saudi Arabia, the UAE, India, Pakistan, China, Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Germany, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom and the United States.
